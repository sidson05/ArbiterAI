# SILVER CARE HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY
**Policy Identifier:** POL-HLT-001  
**Product Category:** Individual Health Insurance  
**Underwriter:** Apex Shield General Insurance Corp  
**Effective Period:** 1 Year Renewable  

---

## Section 1: Inpatient Hospitalization Benefits

### Clause 1.1: Room Rent and Boarding
The Company shall indemnify reasonable and customary room rent, boarding expenses, and nursing charges up to 1.5% of the Sum Insured per day for standard room accommodation. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) charges are covered up to 3% of the Sum Insured per day.

### Clause 1.2: Medical Practitioner and Surgeon Fees
Fees charged by medical practitioners, surgeons, anesthesiologists, and specialists directly attending to the Insured Person during hospitalization are indemnified up to the actual amount or 25% of the total claim value, whichever is lower.

### Clause 1.3: Diagnostic and Therapeutic Procedures
Laboratory tests, X-rays, MRI scans, CT scans, blood transfusions, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and dialysis incurred during continuous hospitalization exceeding 24 hours are covered in full subject to the overall Sum Insured.

---

## Section 2: Pre and Post Hospitalization

### Clause 2.1: Pre-Hospitalization Medical Expenses
Medical expenses incurred within 30 days immediately preceding the date of hospital admission are covered, provided that such expenses are related to the same condition for which hospitalization occurred.

### Clause 2.2: Post-Hospitalization Follow-up Expenses
Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days immediately following the date of discharge from the hospital are reimbursable, limited strictly to follow-up consultations and prescription medications directly related to the treated ailment.

---

## Section 3: Sub-Limits and Deductibles

### Clause 3.1: Compulsory Claim Deductible
A standard compulsory co-payment deductible of $250.00 applies to every individual hospitalization claim filed under this policy.

### Clause 3.2: Cataract Surgery Sub-Limit
Indemnity for cataract surgery per eye is strictly capped at $1,200.00 or actual expenses, whichever is lower.

### Clause 3.3: Joint Replacement Surgery Cap
Surgical procedures involving total or partial knee or hip replacement are subject to a specific sub-limit of $6,500.00 per joint, inclusive of prosthetic implants.

---

## Section 4: General Exclusions

### Clause 4.1: Pre-existing Conditions Waiting Period
Any illness, condition, or ailment diagnosed or treated within 36 months prior to the initial policy inception date is strictly excluded from coverage until the Insured Person has maintained continuous renewal for 36 consecutive months.

### Clause 4.2: Cosmetic and Aesthetic Treatments
Surgery or treatments aimed at aesthetic modification, cosmetic alterations, weight reduction, rhinoplasty, or gender reassignment are permanently excluded, unless directly necessitated by traumatic accidental injury sustained during the policy period.

### Clause 4.3: Dental Surgeries and Procedures
Dental treatments, orthodontics, crowns, and tooth extraction are excluded, except when required as an emergency intervention arising from an accidental injury requiring minimum 24-hour hospitalization.

### Clause 4.4: Self-Inflicted Injuries and Substance Abuse
Hospitalization resulting directly or indirectly from intentional self-harm, attempted suicide, or acute intoxication through non-prescribed narcotics, alcohol, or illicit substances is completely excluded.
