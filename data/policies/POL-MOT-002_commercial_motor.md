# COMMERCIAL GOODS VEHICLE INSURANCE POLICY
**Policy Identifier:** POL-MOT-002  
**Product Category:** Commercial Fleet & Logistics  
**Underwriter:** Apex Shield General Insurance Corp  
**Effective Period:** 1 Year  

---

## Section 1: Fleet Own Damage Coverage

### Clause 1.1: Road Accidents and Transit Collision
Covers accidental damage incurred during freight transit on designated arterial road networks, including loading and unloading incidents.

### Clause 1.2: Vandalism and Riot Protection
Protects against intentional physical damage caused by strikes, civil unrest, mob riots, and malicious acts by third parties.

---

## Section 2: Deductibles and Load Sub-Limits

### Clause 2.1: Commercial Fleet Deductible
A compulsory fleet deductible of $750.00 is levied on all collision claims.

### Clause 2.2: Overloading Penalties and Sub-Limits
If the commercial vehicle was carrying freight exceeding statutory Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW) by more than 10% at the moment of the incident, claim indemnity is reduced by a mandatory 40% non-compliance penalty.

---

## Section 3: Exclusions

### Clause 3.1: Hazardous Chemical Spillage Without Endorsement
Damage resulting from transporting flammable chemicals, toxic industrial waste, or explosives without pre-authorized hazardous cargo endorsement riders is completely excluded.

### Clause 3.2: Unattended Vehicle Theft
Theft of the vehicle or freight resulting from the driver leaving the ignition keys inside the unattended cabin is strictly excluded.
