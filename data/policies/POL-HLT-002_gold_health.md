# GOLD PREMIER HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY
**Policy Identifier:** POL-HLT-002  
**Product Category:** Comprehensive Health & Critical Illness  
**Underwriter:** Apex Shield General Insurance Corp  
**Effective Period:** 1 Year Renewable  

---

## Section 1: Inpatient Hospitalization Benefits

### Clause 1.1: Single Private Suite Accommodation
Indemnity for room rent, nursing charges, and suite boarding without any daily sub-limit up to the single private air-conditioned room category. Full ICU charges are covered without capping.

### Clause 1.2: Modern Robotic and Advanced Surgeries
Coverage extends to robotic surgeries, stem cell therapies, deep brain stimulation, and targeted biological immunotherapy treatments up to 75% of the overall Sum Insured.

### Clause 1.3: Day Care Surgical Treatments
Medical procedures and surgical interventions requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization due to modern technological advancements (e.g., endoscopies, minor orthopedic keyhole surgeries) are covered in full.

---

## Section 2: Extended Benefits & Mental Health

### Clause 2.1: Mental Health Care Support
Inpatient psychiatric hospitalization and clinical psychological evaluation are covered up to a maximum aggregate limit of $5,000.00 per policy year.

### Clause 2.2: Maternity and Newborn Care
Maternity expenses for normal delivery are covered up to $3,500.00 and cesarean sections up to $5,000.00, applicable only after a 12-month waiting period from continuous enrollment.

---

## Section 3: Deductibles and Waiting Periods

### Clause 3.1: Zero Standard Deductible
This Premier plan has no standard co-payment or claim deductible for network hospitals. A $100.00 administrative fee applies to out-of-network claims.

### Clause 3.2: Reduced Pre-Existing Condition Waiting Period
Pre-existing conditions documented at policy enrollment are covered after a 12-month continuous coverage waiting period.

---

## Section 4: Exclusions

### Clause 4.1: Unproven and Experimental Therapies
Any drug, surgical procedure, or medical protocol not approved by statutory medical regulatory authorities (FDA/EMA/National Health Agency) is excluded.

### Clause 4.2: High-Risk Hazardous Sports
Injuries sustained while participating in professional motorsport racing, mountaineering requiring ropes, skydiving, or deep scuba diving exceeding 30 meters are not indemnified.
