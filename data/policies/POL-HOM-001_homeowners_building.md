# HOMEOWNERS COMPREHENSIVE BUILDING INSURANCE POLICY
**Policy Identifier:** POL-HOM-001  
**Product Category:** Residential Real Estate Property  
**Underwriter:** Apex Shield General Insurance Corp  
**Effective Period:** 1 Year Renewable  

---

## Section 1: Property Physical Damage Coverage

### Clause 1.1: Fire, Lightning, and Explosion
The Company indemnifies physical destruction or structural damage to the main residential dwelling, permanent boundary walls, garage, and foundation caused directly by fire, lightning strike, domestic gas cylinder explosion, or aerial projectile impact.

### Clause 1.2: Storm, Flood, and Natural Disasters
Covers damage caused by severe tempest, torrential flash flood, storm water overflow, hurricane, cyclone, or tidal surges, subject to immediate local hydrological weather report verification.

### Clause 1.3: Bursting of Water Pipes and Heating Apparatus
Indemnifies structural water damage directly caused by sudden, accidental bursting, freezing, or overflowing of fixed internal domestic water tanks, plumbing apparatus, or central heating radiators.

---

## Section 2: Financial Conditions and Deductibles

### Clause 2.1: Property Claim Deductible
Each property damage claim is subject to an initial excess deductible of $500.00 payable by the homeowner.

### Clause 2.2: Alternative Temporary Accommodation Allowance
In the event that the insured property becomes uninhabitable following an insured loss, the Company will reimburse reasonable alternative rental accommodation costs up to $2,000.00 per month for a maximum duration of 6 consecutive months.

---

## Section 3: General Exclusions

### Clause 3.1: Gradual Seepage and Long-Term Wear
Damage caused by continuous, gradual water seepage, persistent dampness, foundation settling, subsidence due to soil erosion, building defects, dry rot, or lack of customary maintenance is excluded.

### Clause 3.2: Vacant or Unoccupied Property
Any loss occurring while the insured property has remained continuously vacant or unoccupied for a period exceeding 45 consecutive days prior to the incident is excluded, unless previously notified and endorsed in writing.

### Clause 3.3: Unauthorized Structural Alterations
Collapse or damage arising during or as a direct consequence of unapproved major structural demolition, wall load removal, or excavation carried out without certified civil engineering permits is strictly excluded.
