# COMPREHENSIVE PRIVATE MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY
**Policy Identifier:** POL-MOT-001  
**Product Category:** Private Passenger Vehicle  
**Underwriter:** Apex Shield General Insurance Corp  
**Effective Period:** 1 Year  

---

## Section 1: Loss of or Damage to the Insured Vehicle

### Clause 1.1: Accidental External Collision Damage
The Company will indemnify the Insured against accidental loss of or physical damage to the vehicle caused by external collision, overturn, impact with foreign objects, or impact while in transit by rail, road, inland waterway, or elevator.

### Clause 1.2: Fire, Explosion, and Lightning
Loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidental fire, internal electrical self-ignition, lightning, or external explosion is indemnified up to the Insured Declared Value (IDV).

### Clause 1.3: Natural Perils and Storms
Damage resulting from flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, tempest, inundation, cyclone, hailstorm, earthquake, landslide, or rockslide is covered in full minus the compulsory deductible.

### Clause 1.4: Theft, Burglary, and Total Loss
In the event of total loss through criminal theft, burglary, or housebreaking, compensation equals the full Insured Declared Value (IDV) minus salvage, provided a verified police First Information Report (FIR) is submitted within 48 hours.

---

## Section 2: Deductibles and Depreciation Rules

### Clause 2.1: Compulsory Policy Excess / Deductible
A compulsory deductible of $350.00 shall be deducted from every individual own-damage claim settlement.

### Clause 2.2: Depreciation Scale on Replaced Parts
For partial damage claims requiring replacement of components:
- Rubber, nylon, plastic parts, batteries, airbags: 50% depreciation deduction.
- Glass components: 0% depreciation deduction (indemnified at 100%).
- Fiber glass components: 30% depreciation deduction.
- Metallic body parts: Scaled depreciation based on vehicle age (10% to 40%).

---

## Section 3: General Exclusions

### Clause 3.1: Intoxication and Under the Influence
The Company is not liable for any accident or damage sustained while the insured vehicle is driven by any person under the influence of intoxicating liquor, alcohol, or illicit narcotics.

### Clause 3.2: Unlicensed and Unauthorized Drivers
Any loss or liability occurring while the vehicle is being operated by an individual who does not hold a valid, active driving license recognized by legal motor transit authorities is strictly excluded.

### Clause 3.3: Consequential Mechanical or Electrical Breakdown
Mechanical breakdown, internal engine failure, wear and tear, metal fatigue, rust, corrosion, or damage caused solely by operating the vehicle with low lubricant/coolant levels is excluded.

### Clause 3.4: Commercial Use or Speed Racing
Damage occurring while the private vehicle is employed for commercial hire, fare carriage, courier dispatch, speed testing, or organized rally racing is explicitly excluded.
