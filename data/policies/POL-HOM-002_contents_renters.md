# HOUSEHOLD CONTENTS & RENTERS PROTECTION POLICY
**Policy Identifier:** POL-HOM-002  
**Product Category:** Residential Contents & Personal Property  
**Underwriter:** Apex Shield General Insurance Corp  
**Effective Period:** 1 Year  

---

## Section 1: Covered Possessions and Perils

### Clause 1.1: Burglary and Violent Theft
Indemnifies loss of furniture, household electronic appliances, apparel, and personal belongings resulting from unlawful forced entry or violent burglary into the premises.

### Clause 1.2: Accidental Electrical Damage to Home Appliances
Covers sudden electrical burnout or voltage surge damage to household electrical devices (refrigerators, televisions, washing machines, computers) less than 5 years old.

---

## Section 2: Limits and Specific Valuations

### Clause 2.1: Single Item Limit and Valuables Sub-Cap
The maximum payout for any single piece of art, collectible, or high-value electronic device is limited to $2,500.00. Jewelry, precious metals, and watches are subject to an aggregate sub-limit of $3,500.00 unless accompanied by certified professional appraisal certificates.

### Clause 2.2: Standard Contents Deductible
A flat deductible of $200.00 applies to each individual contents claim settlement.

---

## Section 3: Exclusions

### Clause 3.1: Mysterious Disappearance and Unforced Theft
Theft of personal belongings without physical signs of forcible and violent entry, or unexplained loss, misplaced jewelry, and items left in open yards or balconies is excluded.

### Clause 3.2: Portable Items Damaged Away From Residence
Loss or damage to personal items occurring outside the registered geographical residential address is excluded under this basic contents plan unless covered by an active Worldwide Personal Effects Rider.
